# Access Allies: community card suggestion form

This document is a build-ready specification for a Google Form. Collect **one card idea per response**. The form accepts both Ally and One-shot ideas; suggested gameplay numbers are optional drafts for the team to balance. It does not create cards automatically.

## Form title and description

**Title:** Suggest an Access Allies card

**Description (paste into the form):**

> Access Allies is a card adventure about disabled and neurodivergent animal allies sharing support and overcoming ableist barriers. Suggest one original Ally or One-shot card per response. Allies are animal friends who join the Ally Circle; One-shots are helpful items or activities used once when played. Tell us the idea even if you are unsure about gameplay numbers. The team may edit suggestions for fit, balance, accessibility, and respectful representation; submitting an idea does not guarantee its inclusion. Please do not include anyone's private health information.

## Build order and routing

Create four sections in this order. The form description above appears before Section 1. Mark only the questions labeled **Required** as required.

1. **Card concept** — common questions. Put **Card type** last in this section. On that multiple-choice question, enable **Go to section based on answer**: `Ally` goes to Section 2; `One-shot` goes to Section 3.
2. **Ally details** — set **After section 2** to **Go to section 4: Artwork and contributor details**.
3. **One-shot details** — set **After section 3** to **Go to section 4: Artwork and contributor details**.
4. **Artwork and contributor details** — end with **Submit form**.

This arrangement ensures that either card type reaches the common final section without seeing the other type's questions. [Google Forms section routing instructions](https://support.google.com/docs/answer/141062?hl=en-GB).

## Questions

The text in the **Help text** column should be added as the question description where present. Use the question wording and answer options exactly as shown so the preview checks below are reproducible.

### Section 1 — Card concept

| Question | Type | Required | Help text / options |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Card name | Short answer | Yes | A short name players will see on the card. Example: `Autistic Axolotl` or `Speak Up!` |
| What is the idea behind this card? | Paragraph | Yes | What makes this idea useful, encouraging, or fun in Access Allies? Describe the concept in your own words. |
| Suggested card text | Paragraph | Yes | Draft the short description players would read. It can be rough; the team can edit it. Include what the card does if you know. |
| Suggested Spark cost | Short answer | No | Spark is the energy spent to play a card. Existing examples include `0` for Take a Nap and `2` for Autistic Axolotl. Leave blank if you would like the team to choose. If entered, use a whole number from 0 to 20. |
| Card type | Multiple choice | Yes | **Options:** `Ally`; `One-shot`. An Ally is an animal friend that stays in the Ally Circle. A One-shot is a non-animal item or activity whose effect happens when played. |

### Section 2 — Ally details

| Question | Type | Required | Help text |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| What animal is the Ally? | Short answer | Yes | Name the animal shown on the card. |
| What access need, support, or strength does this Ally portray? | Paragraph | Yes | Explain how the Ally's design centers agency, care, or a useful accommodation. You do not need to disclose your own diagnosis or experience. |
| Suggested Power | Short answer | No | Power is how much an Ally helps against a barrier each round after its first rest. Autistic Axolotl has 3 Power. Leave blank if unsure; if entered, use a whole number from 0 to 100. |
| Suggested Heart | Short answer | No | Heart is how much damage an Ally can take before resting. Autistic Axolotl has 3 Heart. Leave blank if unsure; if entered, use a whole number from 1 to 100. |
| Any effect when played? | Paragraph | No | Most Allies can rely on Power and Heart alone. If this Ally also does something when played, describe the target and amount. Example: `Deal 2 damage to the barrier when played.` |

### Section 3 — One-shot details

| Question | Type | Required | Help text / options |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| What item or activity does the card show? | Short answer | Yes | One-shots show non-animal items or activities, such as a cup of tea, speaking up, or taking a nap. |
| What happens when played? | Multiple choice | Yes | **Options:** `Damage a barrier`; `Heal Team Heart`; `Heal Allies`; `Gain Spark`; `Draw cards`; built-in `Other` option. |
| Describe the target, effect, and suggested amount | Paragraph | Yes | Say who or what is affected, and describe the result. Include a number if you have one; it is a draft, and the team can balance it. Examples: `Deal 4 damage to the barrier` (Speak Up!) or `Heal Team Heart for 5` (Cup of Tea). If you selected Other, explain the new effect here. |

### Section 4 — Artwork and contributor details

| Question | Type | Required | Help text |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Artwork idea | Paragraph | No | Describe the scene, pose, objects, colors, or mood. A written idea is enough; please do not link to someone else's copyrighted artwork as if it were yours. |
| Representation notes | Paragraph | No | Share anything the team should consider to portray the access need or support respectfully. Avoid personal details about other people. |
| Name for credit | Short answer | No | A name or handle the team may use if this idea is included. Leave blank to remain anonymous. |
| Email for follow-up | Short answer | No | Provide an email only if you are comfortable with the team contacting you about this suggestion. Apply Google Forms' email-address response validation to this optional field. |

## Form settings and response handling

- **Collect email addresses:** Off. The optional follow-up email question above is the only contact field.
- **Limit to 1 response:** Off. Contributors may send multiple ideas as separate responses without being required to sign in for this setting.
- **File uploads:** None. Artwork ideas are text only, avoiding the sign-in requirement for a file-upload question.
- **View results summary:** Off; responses and contact details should stay with the form editors.
- **Confirmation message (paste into the form):** `Thank you for suggesting an Access Allies card! The team will review your idea. You can submit another response for a different card.`
- Review each response as a proposal. The team chooses final card text, Spark cost, Ally Power and Heart, One-shot effect amounts, artwork, and whether to include the idea. Contact contributors only if they supplied an email and clarification is useful.

Google Forms documents the relevant [sharing and one-response settings](https://support.google.com/docs/answer/2839588?hl=en) and [email collection settings](https://support.google.com/docs/answer/139706?hl=en).

## Preview and acceptance checklist

1. In Google Forms preview, choose **Ally**. Confirm that only the Ally details appear, followed by Artwork and contributor details, then Submit.
2. Preview again and choose **One-shot**. Confirm that only the One-shot details appear, followed by the same final section, then Submit.
3. Submit a sample Ally with a name, concept, card text, animal, and support theme, leaving all numbers and contact fields blank. Confirm the response is accepted and contains enough information to draft an Ally.
4. Submit a sample One-shot with a name, concept, card text, item or activity, effect choice, and effect description, leaving numbers and contact fields blank. Confirm the response is accepted and contains enough information to draft a One-shot.
5. Check that neither sample submission requires a Google sign-in because of email collection, response limits, or uploads. Confirm the saved responses and confirmation message look correct before sharing the form.
