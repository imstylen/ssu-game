# Grilling

Map consequential decisions and their prerequisites. Inspect available evidence yourself rather than asking factual questions you can answer. Subagents are not required.

Ask currently answerable independent questions together in a numbered round. Give each a concrete question, meaningful tradeoff, and recommended answer. Phrase questions so yes accepts the recommendation. Defer questions dependent on unanswered choices until the next round. Avoid exhaustive hypothetical edge cases.

Wait for answers before making dependent decisions. Recompute the remaining questions after each round. Challenge contradictions with concrete scenarios; do not re-ask settled questions.

When consequential decisions are settled, summarize the agreement and obtain confirmation before implementation. Explicit acceptance of recommendations that fully describe the work can serve as confirmation; do not add a redundant approval round. Write documents during the interview only when the calling workflow requests it.
